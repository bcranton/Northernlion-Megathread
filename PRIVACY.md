# Privacy Policy

The Northernlion Megathread app ("the app") posts and edits stream discussion
threads in the subreddit it is installed in.

## What the app collects

Nothing about Reddit users. The app doesn't read, store or share usernames,
comments, votes or any other user data.

## What the app stores

In the app's Reddit-hosted storage, the app keeps:

- The ID of the current discussion thread.
- The list of games played on the current stream, and the stream's start and
  end times.
- A short-lived Twitch API access token.

## Third parties

The app requests public data from the Twitch API (`id.twitch.tv`,
`api.twitch.tv`): whether the configured channel is live, its current game, its
top clip and its latest VOD. No Reddit data is sent to Twitch.
